\head{2. Working principle of PPO}
\todo{Outline (about 1 page, equations on their own lines):
\begin{itemize}
\item Markov decision process: state, action, reward, return with discount $\gamma$.
\item Stochastic policy $\pi_\theta(a|s)$: Gaussian, mean from a neural network, std learned.
\item Policy gradient idea: raise the probability of actions that did better than expected.
\item Value network $V_\phi(s)$ and advantage $A = $ return $-$ $V$; GAE with $\lambda$ (one equation).
\item Probability ratio $r_t(\theta)$ and the clipped objective (the key equation of PPO).
\item Why clipping: a cheap trust region; several epochs on one batch of data.
\item Full loss: policy term + value loss + entropy term; these are the curves of the loss figure.
\item Link to my BEng thesis (PPO vs robust MPC), one sentence.
\end{itemize}}

\head{3. Environment}
\todo{Outline (about 3/4 page):
\begin{itemize}
\item Hidden world: validated KR16 direct model (Part I); integrates $q \leftarrow q + \dot q\,\Delta t$, $\Delta t = 0.05$ s, 100 steps per episode, joint limits clipped.
\item Action: $\dot q = a \cdot 0.5 \cdot v_{max}$, $a \in [-1,1]^6$.
\item Observation table: with q / without q; tool pose, target pose, error, previous action, multi-scale error (direction, $\log_{10}$ of the norm, $\tanh(e/s)$ for $s=1,0.1,0.01$ m).
\item Reward: log-progress (scale-free, equation), action penalty, $+1$ and stop at success.
\item Start and target: random joint vectors inside the limits, target pose = forward model of it (always reachable).
\item Why the two scales problem (1 m vs 1 mm) and the three tricks used: log reward, multi-scale observation, (optional) tolerance curriculum.
\end{itemize}}
